Road Maintenance & Hazard Reporting
Reason to Call: Pothole or Road Hazard
Report potholes, debris, or pavement damage on state-maintained highways. DOTD is only responsible for state highways — potholes on city streets or parish roads should be referred to local government. Get the route number, nearest mile marker or cross street, and direction of travel so the correct district crew can be dispatched. Check 511LA first to avoid a duplicate report.
📞 1-877-452-3683 (1-877-4LADOTD)

Traffic & Travel Information
Reason to Call: Road Conditions, Closures, or Construction
Direct callers to 511LA, DOTD's free real-time traveler information service, for traffic incidents, closures, construction, weather-related conditions, and commercial vehicle restrictions statewide. Available by website, mobile app, or by dialing 511 from any phone in Louisiana. Callers can create a free My511 account for route-specific alerts. For a specific project, ask for the route or project name.
📞 Dial 511 (in-state) or visit 511LA online
“Is I-10 westbound closed today because of construction?” “Where can I see traffic cameras or closures near Baton Rouge?”

Permits & Commercial Vehicle Services
Reason to Call: Oversize / Overweight Truck Permit
Carriers moving loads that exceed legal length, width, height, or weight limits need a permit from DOTD's Oversized and Overweight Truck Permits office. Options include Single Trip permits (e.g., a $10 mobile home permit, or overweight priced by GVW/mileage) as well as Monthly, Semi-Annual, and Annual permits for repeat carriers. The office runs 7:00 a.m.–3:30 p.m. Monday–Friday, with a 24/7 emergency on-call line for carriers already in transit.
📞 225-343-2345 or 1-800-654-1433 (opt. 1)  |  ✉ DOTDLaGeauxPermits@LA.GOV
“I need a permit to haul a mobile home wider than the legal limit.” “What does it cost to get an overweight permit for my truck?”
