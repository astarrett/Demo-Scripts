Unemployment Claims Document Upload

To process your claim, we may need you to submit one or more documents. You can upload photos or scans of your documents using this secure portal.

1. Identity Verification

Upload one document that proves your identity.
Acceptable documents:
Driver's License, State ID, or Passport
SCAN / UPLOAD

2. Proof of Address

Upload a document that shows your current address.
Acceptable documents:
Utility Bill, Lease Agreement, Bank Statement, or Government Mail
SCAN / UPLOAD

3. Employment Information

Upload documents related to your last employment.
Acceptable documents:
Separation Notice, Layoff Notice, or Letter from Employer
SCAN / UPLOAD

4. Wage Verification (If Applicable)

Upload documents that show your recent earnings.
Acceptable documents:
Pay Stubs, W-2, or 1099
SCAN / UPLOAD

5. Additional Documents (If Applicable)

Upload any other documents requested for your claim.
Examples:
Training records, separation details, or other claim-related documents
SCAN / UPLOAD
Secure & Private
Your documents are encrypted and used only for unemployment insurance purposes.
